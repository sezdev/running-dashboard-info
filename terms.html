Terms of use

Running Dashboard is a personal desktop project. This website provides information about the application and its data practices.

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Purpose and availability

The application helps its owner view and compare information from accounts they are authorized to access. It is not a public health-data service. This website does not offer account registration or hosted access to the owner's dashboard.

The application is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted access, complete provider history or error-free calculations. Provider changes, expired authorization and network failures can interrupt synchronization.

Account access and optional uploads

Connect only accounts you are authorized to use. Review the provider's consent screen before allowing access. Optional weight exports require the relevant permissions and an enabled destination in the local application. Review these settings before syncing.

Data and third-party services

The privacy policy explains how application data is accessed, stored, used and deleted. Connected providers operate under their own terms and policies. The owner remains responsible for their accounts, computer access and separately created backups.

Contact

Questions about this personal project can be directed to the maintainer on GitHub.
