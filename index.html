About Running Dashboard

Running Dashboard helps its owner review recorded runs, planned workouts, fitness trends, personal goals and body measurements. It can connect to Garmin, Strava, Runna, Xiaomi and Google Health through the local application.

This public website provides app information and policies. It does not provide access to the dashboard, connect visitors' accounts, or display personal measurements.

01 / GOOGLE HEALTH

Daily movement, in context

With the owner's consent, the local app reads daily Google Health step counts and total distance. These records remain separate from recorded running activities.

02 / OPTIONAL EXPORT

Weight updates by choice

The owner can separately permit the app to upload selected Xiaomi weight readings to Google Health. This setting is controlled in the local dashboard's Data sources page.

03 / LOCAL STORAGE

Saved on the owner's computer

The app stores account tokens and cached provider records locally, protected using the owner's Windows account. Sync communicates directly with the selected providers.

04 / CLEAR POLICIES

Understand the permissions

The privacy policy explains the data accessed, its purpose, storage, deletion and account controls.
